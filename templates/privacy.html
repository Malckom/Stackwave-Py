    Who we are

    This website is run by Stackwave Developers, a business name registered in Kenya (registration number {{ site.business_reg_no }}), based in Nairobi. We decide how and why the personal information described here is used, which makes us the data controller under the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

    You can reach us about anything in this policy at {{ site.email }}.

    What we collect

    WhenWhat we collect
    You send a message through the contact formYour name, email address, the service you are interested in, your message, and any package or add-ons you selected on the Pricing page.
    You complete the project onboarding formYour company name, website address, main goal, notes, and any files you choose to upload.
    You contact us by email, phone or WhatsAppThe details you share with us in that conversation, including your contact details.
    You use the websiteA one-way, salted hash of your IP address that we use only to limit spam on our forms (we do not store the IP address itself), and a session cookie described below. Our web host may also keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested.

    We do not ask for sensitive personal information through this website, and we ask that you do not send any.

    How we use it

    • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
    • To plan and deliver a project you have asked us to do, and to manage invoicing and payments.
    • To protect our website and forms from spam and misuse.
    • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

    We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

    Our lawful basis

    We rely on the following grounds under the Data Protection Act, 2019:

    • Your consent, when you choose to send us a message or an onboarding form.
    • Steps before and during a contract, when we prepare a quote or deliver work for you.
    • Our legitimate interests, such as keeping our website secure and free of spam.
    • Legal obligations, such as keeping financial records.

    Cookies and fonts

    We use one essential session cookie. It keeps multi-step forms working and helps protect our forms from forged requests. It does not track you across other sites. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first.

    This website loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and browser details under its own privacy policy.

    Who we share it with

    We share personal information only with the services we need to run the website and deliver your project, and only as much as they need:

    • Our web hosting provider, which stores the website and its data.
    • Email and messaging providers, when we correspond with you (for example email and WhatsApp).
    • Domain, hosting and payment providers, when you ask us to set these up for a project.
    • Professional advisers or authorities, where the law requires it.

    Some of these providers may store information on servers outside Kenya. Where that happens, we take the steps the Data Protection Act, 2019 requires to keep your information protected.

    How long we keep it

    We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond to you and, if you become a client, for the length of the project and the period we must keep financial records. If an enquiry does not lead to a project, we delete or anonymize it within 24 months of our last contact. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

    Your rights

    Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

    • be told how your personal information is used;
    • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
    • object to us using your information in certain ways;
    • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
    • ask us to delete your information; and
    • ask for your information in a portable format.

    To use any of these rights, email {{ site.email }}. We will respond as soon as we can, and within the time limits set by Kenyan law. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

    Security

    We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including HTTPS, limited access to stored enquiries, and protections against spam and forged form submissions. No online service is completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through the website.

    Children

    Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as our website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
